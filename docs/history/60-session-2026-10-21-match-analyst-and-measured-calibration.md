# Session 2026-10-21 — the Match Analyst, measured signal reliability, calibrated adjustments and prices

User, verbatim: *"make more MASSIVE UPGRADES TO THE DASHBOARD, OPTIMIZER, WHATEVER ... state of the art techniques, aesthetics and ideas that are actually useful. Qualitative ai needs to be massively upgraded as well."*

The five queued full-time analyses were written first (GW8 week, matches 5795475/77/80/83/84). Writing them by hand showed what the qualitative layer was missing, and the session turned that into code.

## What was built

**The Match Analyst (`models/match_analyst.py`, migration 0049 `match_story`).** A finished match read from stored shots, goals, substitutions and FPL status changes, with no network and no language model: the score timeline (own goals found where the scorer's club is not the credited club), the result the shots deserve (`match_rebuild.deserved_result`), xG by situation and zone for and against with the league mix as yardstick, minutes and chances in each game state, the densest ten-minute spell, every substitution classified (injury-linked only when `change_events` shows FPL moving the player off "available" within 48 hours of kickoff), one line per notable player, and ranked findings in the schema `apply_match_analysis` already takes. Built for all 70 finished matches, rebuilt in `run-scheduled` (`refresh_stories`) for any new full-time match, shown on `/api/match` as `story`, and printed at the top of `fpl match-report`. `fpl match-story <id> --draft FILE` writes the findings, `player_states` and `team_states` as a payload; the five queued analyses were regenerated from it. The `match-intelligence-analysis` skill now starts from it.

Things it found that the hand-written versions missed: Robertson (69'), Tosin (71') and Marmoush (87') all came off at Spurs and were flagged doubtful by FPL that night; Martinez (46') at United; Danso suspended. A 46' substitution had been read as a half-time swap.

**Signal reliability (`models/signal_reliability.py`, `/api/signals`, Football screen).** For each match-level metric, the correlation between a side's or player's prior average (three or more matches) and its next match, with a Fisher-z 90% interval: this season's shot-level metrics (80 pairs) and, where Understat carries them, every stored season (3,580 team-match pairs, 18,245 player-match pairs). Results: a player's shots per 90 r +0.56 and xG per 90 +0.48 persistent; team xG for +0.33 and against +0.26 persistent; share of xG from headers +0.29 (persistent, 80 pairs); dead-ball share of xG +0.16 and conceded +0.21 unclear; goals above xG +0.03 and goals conceded above xG against +0.00 do not carry. Each analyst finding that rests on one of these carries the number and is capped at low confidence when the verdict is "none" or "too few".

**Measured streak calibration (`backtesting/streak_calibration.py`, `fpl streak-study`).** `qualitative_feed` added 35% of a goals or assists component for every persistent trend. Against a flat empirical-Bayes season rate that already contains the streak (22,083 Understat player-matches), realised/baseline was 1.066 for a goal-threat streak (90% interval 0.99-1.14), 0.777 for two 60+ minute matches with no shot, 1.192 for a creation streak on assists and 0.797 for two matches with no key pass. `CALIBRATED_FRACTIONS` now sizes the adjustment: goal threat and role change +5% / -20%, creation +20% / -20%; set-piece signals keep 0.35 because the history has no taker data. This lowers the positive goal-threat bump five-fold and changes projections: the GW8 plan went from "Palmer -> Saka" plus one other move at +5.1 to Palmer -> Saka and Gibbs-White -> Mbeumo at +4.2 (`fpl week-plan --no-save`). The minutes adjustment (`expected_minutes`, still 0.35) was checked against this season's GW minutes: players with two straight 60+ minute matches and a season mean under 60 played 1.35x that mean next time, so it stays.

**The price model (`models/price_model.py`, `fpl price-model`).** The old momentum flag (net transfers above 0.5% of players = "price rising") was right on 5.3% of the nights it fired for a rise and 3.3% for a fall on the stored history (780 flagged rise-nights, 41 actual rises). Two small L2 logistic regressions on net transfers over 24 hours, net since the deadline and log ownership now give tonight's probability; the rise model scores AUC 0.97 and Brier skill +0.04 on the last 14 nights (28 moves), the fall model does not beat the base rate (AUC 0.61, skill -0.03) and is not used. Nobody has more than a 5% rise chance tonight, so "do this early because of price" no longer fires for Mbeumo. Refit daily in `run-scheduled`, stored in `app_meta['price_model']`.

**Previews carry a read of the form (`models/team_reading.py`).** Each upcoming fixture lists, for each club, chances for and against over its last five stories, points against deserved points (with the persistence caveat attached), injury-linked flags still open, and the dead-ball matchup when one side's attack and the other's defence both run through dead balls.

**Frontend.** Match page: the analyst's read, a deserved-result bar, a scrubbable shot replay (both sides on one pitch, cumulative xG, game-state band, goals, substitutions, cards, play and arrow keys), game state, situation mix, substitutions read against the score, findings with persistence tags. Table: xPts and luck columns, sortable. Football: the signal ledger. Player file: what the analyst saw. Previews: the read. Command, Desk, Scout: price probabilities. Every screen except Command is now its own chunk: first load fell from 2.5 MB to 473 KB of JavaScript.

## Defects found on the way
- **Overturned goals were stored as goals (7 matches).** FotMob reuses an event id when VAR rules a goal out: the incident first arrives as type "Goal", then as type "VAR". `sync_match`'s upsert updated the description and not `event_type`, so the match report timeline, the live charts and the new analyst all showed a phantom goal in seven matches (found because the analyst's timeline disagreed with the final score in exactly those seven). Fixed in the upsert, repaired in place by migration 0050, covered by a test that syncs a goal and then the VAR event with the same id.
- **A "Goal ruled out" is still a VAR event the analyst does not describe**: the decision text is not stored, only the player's name.
- Two substitutions in the history still resolve no position for the player coming on (a player FPL now lists at another club).

## Assumptions and limits to keep disclosing
- Shot coordinates are metres on a 105 x 68 pitch (FotMob). Game state is read at minute resolution; stoppage time is folded into 45 and 90.
- Deserved result treats shots as independent. Shot-sum xG differs from FotMob's team xG by up to 0.2 (Brighton 2.20 v 1.98); the story says so.
- Injury-linked needs an FPL status change; a withdrawal with no change stays "early unexplained" or "managed minutes".
- The reliability table is one season of shots (80 pairs) beside the multi-season history for the metrics Understat has; the intervals say how much that is worth.
- The streak study scores a flat shrunk rate, not the full projection; players with no 45-minute matches are absent from the history table.
- The price model has 28 rises in its held-out window; skill is measured there. Falls are not modelled.
- Not done: `SET_PIECES`/`SET_PIECE_CHANGE` fractions are untested; the analyst writes `match_observations` on its own only for a finished match nobody has analysed 8 hours after kickoff (confidence capped at medium; `fpl match-analyze` replaces it), because those rows feed projections through `qualitative_feed`; it is post-match only, with no live reading.
